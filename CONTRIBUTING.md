# Contributing to Moodle UX+

Contributions are welcome as **adaptable recipes** rather than unqualified claims of universal compatibility.

A useful contribution should explain:

1. **Problem:** what repetitive Moodle task needs improvement?
2. **Context:** what Moodle/browser setup and permissions were involved (without revealing private site details)?
3. **Discovery:** what safe structural facts must a human–AI team verify?
4. **Approach:** UI behaviour, state, and network/data access.
5. **Testing:** what was actually verified, and what remains uncertain?
6. **Safety:** what permissions/data are required, and how can users disable the feature?

Please do not submit credentials, tokens, real student/staff records, institutional domains, raw authenticated HTML, or sensitive screenshots. Replace any sample data with synthetic values. Describe assumptions explicitly. Avoid unsolicited large-scale refactors or features that change records without confirmation.

A good issue title might be: `Recipe: Jump from a user profile to their enrolled courses`.
